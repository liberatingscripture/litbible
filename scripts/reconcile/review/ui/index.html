Reconciliation review

Each highlighted span is one difference between the Word master and the repo. Choose a side per span. Grey spans are pre-answered: mechanical ones (diacritics, quote characters, spacing) take the master, structural ones (markup only, no word changes) keep the repo. The amber spans are the real questions.

Decisions save as you make them, to scripts/reconcile/out/decisions.json. Apply them with node scripts/reconcile/apply.mjs --decision=approved --write.
