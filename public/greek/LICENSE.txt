The Greek tab's data. Nothing from the LIT is in these files.

text/
  The Greek New Testament word by word, one file per chapter.
  The text is the SBL Greek New Testament (SBLGNT), edited by Michael W.
  Holmes, copyright 2010 Society of Biblical Literature and Logos Bible
  Software, CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/;
  https://sblgnt.com). Lemmas and parsing are from MorphGNT: SBLGNT Edition,
  ed. J. K. Tauber (https://github.com/morphgnt/sblgnt), CC BY-SA 3.0, with
  Strong's numbers and the parsing of John 7:53-8:11 and Romans 16:25-27
  from MACULA Greek
  (Clear Bible, https://github.com/Clear-Bible/macula-greek), CC BY 4.0. The
  Greek of Romans 16:25-27 is from the SBLGNT apparatus.
  Sense numbers point into the UBS dictionary below.
  This directory is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.

ubs/
  Entries of the UBS Dictionary of New Testament Greek, © United Bible
  Societies, 2023. Adapted from Semantic Dictionary of Biblical Greek:
  © United Bible Societies 2018-2023, which is adapted from Greek-English
  Lexicon of the New Testament: Based on Semantic Domains, Eds. J P Louw,
  Eugene Albert Nida © United Bible Societies 1988, 1989. CC BY-SA 4.0
  (https://github.com/ubsicap/ubs-open-license). Inline markup is reduced to
  plain text. This directory is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.

step/
  The Translators Brief lexicon of Extended Strongs for Greek (TBESG), from
  STEPBible.org, Tyndale House Cambridge, CC BY 4.0, based on Abbott-Smith
  (public domain). Reformatted for the Greek tab: markup reduced to bold,
  italic and line breaks, and references made plain text.
  Where Abbott-Smith has no entry, STEPBible fills in Middle Liddell, and
  those entries end "(ML)". Middle Liddell comes from the Perseus Digital
  Library, Tufts University (https://github.com/PerseusDL/lexica), CC BY-SA,
  so each entry ending "(ML)" is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. The rest of this
  directory is CC BY 4.0.

lsj/
  The Translators Formatted full LSJ lexicon, from STEPBible.org, Tyndale
  House Cambridge, edited from Liddell, Scott and Jones. STEPBible takes its
  LSJ text from the Perseus Digital Library, Tufts University
  (https://github.com/PerseusDL/lexica), CC BY-SA, so this directory is
  licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, with Tyndale House's additions (CC BY 4.0) under it.
  Corrections to its wording are offered to the Perseus Digital Library.
  Reformatted for the Greek tab: markup reduced to bold, italic and line
  breaks, references made plain text, and each source reference kept as
  hover text.

  STEPBible asks that its data be taken from its own source, where
  corrections arrive, rather than passed on by others. These two
  directories are formatted for the Greek tab and are not a copy of the
  data: to use the lexicons yourself, get them from
  https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data (CC BY 4.0).

search/
  The index behind searching the Greek: for each lemma, its forms (letters
  only, accents and breathings removed), Strong's numbers and gloss, and the
  place and parsing of each of its words (lemmas.json, occ/), and the same
  words grouped by parsing (parse.json, parse/). Built from MorphGNT's lemmas
  and parsing (CC BY-SA 3.0), with Strong's numbers from MACULA Greek (Clear
  Bible, CC BY 4.0). A lemma's glosses are the UBS dictionary's above (CC
  BY-SA 4.0) and, for most lemmas, the brief gloss of STEPBible's TBESG
  lexicon (Tyndale House Cambridge, CC BY 4.0). As with step/ and lsj/,
  those glosses are formatted for this search and are not a copy of the
  data: to use the lexicon yourself, get it from
  https://github.com/STEPBible/STEPBible-Data (CC BY 4.0). Where a lemma's
  step/ entry ends "(ML)" (Middle Liddell, above) and the brief gloss given
  here is the same as that entry's, the gloss is treated as CC BY-SA 4.0
  like the entry: the cautious route, until STEPBible confirms in writing
  that it is not Perseus's text. Apart from the other glosses, this
  directory is licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
