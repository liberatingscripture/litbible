The Liberation & Inclusion Translation Newsletter
Now a work of the Liberating Scripture Collective
Happy Pentecost! This weekend we celebrated the day language and ethnic barriers were decisively crossed and God poured about the Sacred Life-breath publicly on all regardless of those historical markers of who belongs and who is "other." The verdict? Everyone belongs!
 
Honoring that foundation of the community of people who honor and follow Jesus' teachings of love, justice, and liberation, it seems fitting to announce a new chapter in development of the Liberation and Inclusion Translation.
For years, you've journeyed with me (Brandon) as I've translated the LIT Bible and explored how it has formed my understanding and surfaced justice and liberation as Brandon Rhodes and I have produced the Found in Translation Podcast.
Now, we're finding it time to expand the work and invite others into the community doing that work. We're excited to share a new chapter with you, one that solidifies the foundation we've built and opens the door for others to participate and help us build even more.
We are thrilled to introduce The Liberating Scripture Collective, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. This is the official home for the LIT Bible and all the surrounding resources you've come to know and love. Our mission remains the same: to make the liberating truth of the Bible accessible and live out the reality that everyone belongs.
 
This new structure allows us to deepen our commitment to this mission and expand our offerings. We are excited to explore new initiatives and invite participation and ideas:
•A brand new podcast hosted by a spiritual director and a trauma therapist, inviting guests to explore stories of healing and belonging
•Ways to connect people with spiritual support with spiritual directors
•A community space for deeper connection and shared learning
•Opportunities for participation in the the work LSC is doing and supporting its ongoing projects
Still Free. Now Sustainable.
The heart of the work has always been about open access. The LIT Bible and its core resources will remain completely free, accessible to anyone who seeks them. As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, The Liberating Scripture Collective is now structured for long-term sustainability. Your support allows us to continue creating, curating, and distributing these vital resources without compromising our commitment to open access. We believe everyone deserves the opportunity to engage with scripture in a way that liberates, includes, and heals, and your contribution helps make that a reality.
We invite you to learn more about our vision and how you can be a part of this exciting new chapter.
