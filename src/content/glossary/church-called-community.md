"Church" carries centuries of institutional weight that the Greek word does not. *Ekklesia* literally means "the group called out," those assembled together, having been called out from their homes for something, the way people would be called out for a town council meeting.

It describes a community of people called from within a larger society in order to respond to the needs of that society. Something like a neighborhood association is closer to the sense of it than a building or a denomination.

The word shares its root with *eklektos*, traditionally rendered "chosen" or "elect." That word is not about choosing some people over against rejecting others. It is about calling people for a specific moment or purpose, selecting particular people from among many to participate in something.
