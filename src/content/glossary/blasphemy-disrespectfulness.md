The English word "blasphemy" has come to mean a very particular type of offense against God. However, the Greek word is not nearly as specific as that. It means to speak against something or someone in a way that is disrespectful and causes it to be viewed with less esteem or more judgmentalism. Words like "disrespect," "slander," "defamation," "belittling," "insult" and others like these could work. The Greek word *blasphemia* appears dozens of times in the Bible, and while many instances are about speaking or implying things against God, many are not.
