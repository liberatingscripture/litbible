*Hupokrites* is the ordinary Greek word for a stage actor, someone playing a role. It is related to *prosopon*, which was used of the masks actors wore. The emphasis falls on actions that are performed for an audience.

The English "hypocrite" is close but not identical. It now primarily implies moral inconsistency, saying one thing and doing another. The Greek points at something more specific: someone who outwardly displays devotion in order to be seen doing it, rather than out of genuine faithfulness.

"Pretender" and "pretense" both keep the sense of performance that the traditional rendering has lost.
