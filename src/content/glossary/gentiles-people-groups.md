"Gentiles" is not really a translation. It is a category word that has hardened over time into a label for outsiders, and it tells an English reader almost nothing about what the Greek says.

*Ethnos* literally means "nation," in the sense of nationalities rather than countries, or "people group." It is where the English word "ethnicity" comes from. It is usually, though not always, used to refer to people groups other than the one the speaker belongs to. Since most of the speakers and authors in the New Testament are Jewish, that typically means people who are not Jewish.

But not always, and the exceptions matter. In some letters the audience is largely not Jewish, so the word is being used for people who follow neither Christ nor Torah. In John 11:50 it refers to the people group descended from Jacob, Israel, as a whole.
