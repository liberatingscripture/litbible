The English word 'Gentiles' has strayed from the original meaning. It comes from the Latin word *gentilis* from the root *gens* meaning 'clan' or 'tribe.' It was used to translate the Greek word *ethnos*, which also means something like people group. Most English Bibles translated it as 'Gentile' or 'nation.' It's where the English word 'ethnicity' comes from, and when it's translated as 'nation' it's referring to a group of people (like 'nationality'), not the state, territory, or government. While it is often used about *other* groups of people in contrast with Jews, that is not the definition of the word. In fact, in John 11:50, it was used specifically of Israel. The LIT Bible translates it in various ways based on context, such as 'peoples,' 'people groups,' and 'other people groups.'
